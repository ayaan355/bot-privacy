Privacy Policy

Claude Chat Assistant is a small personal WhatsApp assistant operated by Md Sabuz Mia.
Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Who can use it

The assistant is for private use. It replies only to a short list of phone numbers approved by the operator. Messages from any other number are ignored and are not answered.

2. What information is processed

  • The WhatsApp phone number of the approved sender.
  • The text of the messages that the approved sender sends to the assistant.

3. How it is used

Message text is sent to Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic, only to write a reply. The reply is sent back to the sender through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta.

4. Storage and retention

The assistant keeps recent conversation context only in the memory of the operator's computer so that replies make sense. It is erased when the program restarts. The operator does not build a separate database of messages and does not sell or share personal information with advertisers or other third parties.

5. Third-party services

Messages are handled by Meta (WhatsApp) and Anthropic (Claude) under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Your choices and deletion

An approved sender can stop using the assistant at any time. To ask for removal of your number from the approved list, or to ask any question about your data, contact the operator at the email below.
